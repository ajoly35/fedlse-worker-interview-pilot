2. How the interview works

On the next page, you will talk with an AI interviewer about how AI is, or is not, changing your work. It takes about 8 to 10 minutes and works like a phone call: you answer out loud, in your own words. There are no right or wrong answers, and nothing you say is shared with your employer.

Before you start

  • Find a reasonably quiet place, and use a browser such as Chrome, Safari or Edge.
  • When asked, allow the microphone.
  • Turn off ad blockers or privacy extensions for this site; they can stop the interview from loading.
  • On a phone, so that the screen stays on if you pause, change these settings now, before you start (you can change them back afterwards):
    • iPhone: Settings > Display & Brightness > Auto-Lock > Never, and turn off Low Power Mode (Settings > Battery), which locks the screen after 30 seconds.
    • Android: Settings > Display > Screen timeout > the longest option.

Need to stop? Click Pause at any time, for example if you are called in to an appointment. Your microphone is switched off while paused, and your answers so far are kept. When you are ready, click Resume on the same page and device: the interviewer will carry on where you left off. For a short break, you can also just say "one moment".

While paused, keep this page open and your screen on. On a phone, do not lock it or switch to another app; on a laptop, keep the lid open and plugged in if you can. If the call does end, nothing is lost: click Resume and the interview carries on.

The interview is recorded and transcribed for research.
